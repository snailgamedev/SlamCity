HOW TO FIGHT
🕹️
MOVE — left stick. It's real-time: walk in to land hits, back off to breathe.
👊
JAB fast & cheap · POWER heavy & slow · ✝ FINISH unlocks when they're low.
🛡️
BLOCK (hold) soaks damage · DODGE (tap) i-frames you through a shot.
🎯
Hits land by distance + timing, not luck — out of reach = a whiff.
HP = health (top bar) · STAMINA = powers your attacks, drains when you block, refills over time.
📈
Every fighter has living ratings — Striking · Power · Finisher · Blocking · Stamina · Speed · Heart — that rise & fall by how you fight. Weight class & style matter.
